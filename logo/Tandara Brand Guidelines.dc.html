01

Foundation

Tandara transforms fragmented local signals, preparedness plans, and community reports into clearer shared readiness—without replacing official disaster authorities.

Calm

People open Tandara when they are worried. The brand lowers the temperature with quiet colour, steady type and no alarm graphics.

Source-first

Every piece of information shows where it came from, when, and who reviewed it. Trust must be visible.

Honest about gaps

Unknown is safer than invented. The brand never implies safety, certainty or authority it does not have.

02

Logo

The mark is a ripple. The amber point is the local signal: one observation, one household, one report. The closed ring is the reviewed, shared understanding around it. The outer ring is readiness reaching the wider community, left open because what is known is never complete. The wordmark is lowercase Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, approachable rather than institutional.

03

Colour

Deep water and calm teal carry the brand. Amber is the signal colour: used sparingly, for the mark and the single most important action on a screen. Red is reserved for system errors only. It never appears in the logo or in brand moments, so it keeps its meaning.

04

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans was designed in Jakarta and reads clearly on low-end Android screens. IBM Plex Mono is used only for metadata: timestamps, versions, sources and IDs. That makes the provenance of every card easy to spot. Body text never goes below 16 px for residents.

05

Status labels

Labels are the most important brand element inside the product. Each one pairs an icon, a word and a colour, so the meaning survives for colour-blind users and on greyscale screens. AI and Simulation labels use a dashed border to signal that the content is not verified or not real.

07

Tone of voice

Tandara speaks like a calm neighbour who helps coordinate the RW: plain Bahasa Indonesia, short sentences, specific next steps. It says what is known, what is not, and who said it.

Plain

Everyday words. No jargon, no acronyms without explanation. Readable at primary-school level.

Steady

No exclamation marks, no capitals for emphasis, no urgency words unless an official source uses them.

Accountable

Name the source and the time. Say "we don't know yet" when that is the truth.
